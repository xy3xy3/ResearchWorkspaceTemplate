# 返修工作模板

先填内部台账，再依据已经完成并核对的修改生成回复。未执行的实验不得放进完成态句子。投稿语言按目标要求，表内中文说明不自动进入英文回复信。

## 内部意见台账

| ID | 原始意见 | 实际问题 | 处理与依据 | 正文文件/版本/定位 | 实验/引用证据 | 状态 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1.1 | <保留原意> | <待填> | <修改/澄清/有据不同意> | <待填> | <待填> | pending |

状态：pending / changed / verified / blocked。只有 verified 才能确认回复中对应的已完成动作。最终版排版后复核页/行号。

## 开头

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

Thank you for reviewing our manuscript, [verified manuscript ID and title]. Below, we respond to each comment and identify the corresponding changes in [exact revised version]. [Describe the marking convention only if actually applied.]

## Reviewer [number], Comment [number]

**Comment:** [Original comment, preserving its meaning and any sub-questions.]

**Response — 已核验的修改：**
Thank you for identifying [specific issue]. We have [completed action, not a plan]. [Verified evidence and its interpretation.] We revised [specific claim/text] in Section [X], page [Y], lines [Z], and [table/figure if applicable].

**Response — 部分同意或有据不同意：**
We agree that [valid part]. However, [precise difference in assumptions or evidence, with verified sources]. To avoid ambiguity, we have [actual clarification]. The revised text now limits the claim to [supported scope]. [Do not claim a clarification was made before editing the manuscript.]

**Response — 确实无法完成所需实验：**
We cannot currently perform [requested experiment] because [specific, true constraint]. [Describe an alternative only if actually completed and explain what it does and does not answer.] We have [verified restriction/removal of the unsupported claim]. [State the remaining limitation honestly.]

**Changes:** [Exact short excerpt or precise location; it must match the revised manuscript.]

## 结尾

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.

[Accurate author/communication information]

## 最后核对（不放回复信）

每条意见均有回应；每项承诺对应实际文件/证据；新结果或结论变化同步全文；无虚构实验、无无关引用、无错误页码；作者已确认的事项才写作已确认。回复信不代替清洁稿/标记稿，也不代表录用。
